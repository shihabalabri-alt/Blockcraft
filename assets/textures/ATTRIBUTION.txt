Integration: the game keeps its own procedural 16x16 atlas and its own
engine. At boot these real PNGs are drawn over the matching atlas tiles
(texture keys grass_top, grass_side, dirt, stone, cobble, sand, oak_log,
oak_log_top, oak_leaves, planks, coal_ore, iron_ore, gold_ore,
diamond_ore, redstone_ore, emerald_ore, copper_ore, lapis_ore,
lapis_block, deepslate, deepslate_top, deepslate_lapis_ore). Other
blocks stay procedural. The items/ sprites are used by the survival
inventory, hotbar, crafting results, chests, furnaces, drops and the
Lapis item counter. Block icons are rendered from the patched atlas.
